Echo cancellation
Tips: Keep the guide layers fairly quiet and sing close to the phone's mic (usually the bottom edge). Echo cancellation filters out some of what the speaker plays, which keeps takes cleaner, but it can make your voice sound a little thinner. If the layers stack up into mush, turn off Hear earlier layers and use the starting pitch plus the visual beat instead.
iPhone: if you can't hear anything, flip off the silent switch and turn the volume up.

Timing calibration
Phones add a small delay between the speaker and the mic, which can make takes land a little late. Calibrate once: set the phone on a table in a quiet room, turn the volume up, and tap the button. It plays 10 clicks and listens for them.
